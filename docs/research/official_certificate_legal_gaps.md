# Schobot — Official Certificate Legal-Gap Research (Maharashtra)

**Research checked:** 25 November 2026 (IST)  
**Scope:** The unresolved legal-source questions in `official_certificate_sources.md`. This is source research, not legal advice. No application code, database, CSV, or existing functionality was changed.

## Research method and confidence

Only Maharashtra or Government of India sources are used as evidence. Search results were used to discover official pages, not to establish legal facts. A document is called **verified** only to the extent its government-hosted page or file was opened and its relevant contents inspected. Where a PDF is image-only, inaccessible, or not current enough to support a present-day legal conclusion, that limitation is stated.

## 1. The Income Certificate form's 2012 GR reference — Form G

The official Revenue Department form previously catalogued for Income Certificate carries the reference:

> Sankirna 1/2012/Pr.Kr.18/E-1, Appendix B, Form G

- **Form:** [Application form for Income Certificate](https://revenue.mahaonline.gov.in/Site/Common/Documents/Downloads/Application_form_for_Income_Certificate.pdf)
- **GR search portal:** [Government Resolutions — Maharashtra Government](https://gr.maharashtra.gov.in/1145/Government-Resolutions)
- **Finding:** The form's printed reference is verifiable, but the original standalone GR and its full title, issuing department, date, and PDF URL were **not verified**.
- **Search limitation:** The official GR portal page opened. Its search form asks for filters including department, keyword, date, and GR code, and presents a CAPTCHA. The exact reference was not returned as a directly accessible indexed document. I did not attempt to bypass the CAPTCHA or infer a result.
- **Conclusion:** Do not treat the form itself as the full GR, and do not infer a date or legal rule from the file-number text.

## 2. The Age, Nationality and Domicile form's 2012 GR reference — Form T

The official combined application form carries:

> Sankirna 1/2012/Pr.Kr.18/E-1, Appendix B, Form T

- **Form:** [Application form for Age, Nationality and Domicile Certificate](https://revenue.mahaonline.gov.in/Site/Common/Documents/Downloads/Application_form_for_Age_Nationality_and%20Domicile_Certificate.pdf)
- **GR search portal:** [Government Resolutions — Maharashtra Government](https://gr.maharashtra.gov.in/1145/Government-Resolutions)
- **Form details inspected:** The four-page form asks for the applicant's current and permanent addresses, the year residence began, Maharashtra residence history, migration history, and (where relevant) a spouse's residence history. Its PDF metadata records creation on 15 April 2014 and modification on 16 April 2014.
- **Finding:** The printed Form T citation is verifiable, but the original standalone GR and its full title, issuing department, date, and PDF URL were **not verified**. The official GR portal search limitation is the same as for Form G.
- **Conclusion:** The form is historical evidence of the fields collected, not proof of the complete current rules or a minimum residence threshold.

The shared file-number reference does not establish that the Income and combined certificate services have identical substantive requirements.

## 3. Caste certificate issuance law and rules

### 3.1 Act

The Social Justice and Special Assistance Department's [Acts & Rules catalogue](https://sjsa.maharashtra.gov.in/en/document-category/acts-rules/) lists **Caste Certificate Act, 2000**, dated 23 May 2001, and links an accessible PDF.

- **Issuing authority:** Government of Maharashtra, Law and Judiciary Department (as printed in the Act copy); the catalogue is maintained by the Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.
- **Act number and title verified in the official text:** Maharashtra Act No. XXIII of 2001, *The Maharashtra Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, De-notified Tribes (Vimukta Jatis), Nomadic Tribes, Other Backward Classes and Special Backward Category (Regulation of Issuance and Verification of) Caste Certificate Act, 2000*.
- **Official Act PDF, linked by the department:** [Caste Certificate Act, 2000 (PDF)](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3b090409688550f3cc93f4ed88ec6cafb/uploads/2025/01/2025011333462527.pdf). The 9-page downloaded copy is image-only; text extraction returned no text, so its body was not independently checked page by page.
- **Readable official-law copy inspected:** [Maharashtra Act No. XXIII of 2001 — official Maharashtra-hosted PDF](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s36a4cbdaedcbda0fa8ddc7ea32073c475/uploads/2025/02/20250210421165874.pdf). Its first page states that the text is “As modified upto the 29th October 2012”; the 12-page PDF was opened and its title/version text extracted.
- **Currency caveat:** The 2012 compilation proves the Act's title and that compilation's cut-off only. The department catalogue's undated “Caste Certificate Act, 2000” entry does not itself say that its linked scan incorporates amendments after that date. I did not verify a consolidated Act current through 2026 or establish that no later amendment exists.

### 3.2 Caste Certificate Rules, 2012

The same department catalogue lists **Caste Certificate Rules, 2012**, dated 31 August 2012, and links the [67-page Rules PDF](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3b090409688550f3cc93f4ed88ec6cafb/uploads/2025/01/202501131491187877.pdf).

- **Issuing authority:** Government of Maharashtra, Social Justice and Special Assistance Department.
- **Notification:** CBC10/2012/C.R.165/B.C.W., dated 31 August 2012.
- **Gazette publication:** Maharashtra Government Gazette, Extraordinary, Part IV-B, 3 September 2012 (the Gazette heading visible on the PDF).
- **Relevant contents inspected:** Page 1 gives the department, notification, Act and date. Page 2 defines “deemed date” by category. Page 3, Rule 4, is headed “Procedure for obtaining Caste Certificate from Competent Authority” and says the applicant submits Form 1 with affidavits in Forms 2 and 3.
- **Verification limitation:** This is a scanned PDF. Pages 1–3 were visually inspected in the browser; automated text extraction did not work. The department page and PDF establish the 2012 Rules, but no later Rules amendment or current consolidated Rules text was verified in this research.

The Act's combined title covers issuance and verification. A caste certificate should not be described as the same thing as a caste-validity decision; the official Aaple Sarkar directory separately lists the Caste Certificate Verification Committee service.

## 4. Government resolutions and Maharashtra community lists

### 4.1 Verified state list circular for VJNT, NT, OBC and SBC

The Other Backward Bahujan Welfare Department hosts a 16-page circular titled **Maharashtra State list of Vimukta Jatis, Nomadic Tribes, Other Backward Classes and Special Backward Category**:

- **Direct document:** [Maharashtra state community-list circular (PDF)](https://cdnbbsr.s3waas.gov.in/s3d0ddf516f6050bc8a03ca634498843e4/uploads/2025/08/202508111589085478.pdf)
- **Issuing authority:** Government of Maharashtra, Other Backward Bahujan Welfare Department.
- **Circular number/date:** Sankirna-2024/Pr.Kr.308/Mavak, 9 January 2025.
- **Portal reference number printed in the circular:** 202501091129055634.
- **Verification:** The official department's category page and the linked PDF were opened. The PDF's first pages identify the department, number and date; pages contain the state category lists. PDF metadata records creation on 9 January 2025.
- **What it supports:** A government-published consolidated reference list for VJ-A, NT-B, NT-C, NT-D, OBC and SBC categories. It cites earlier instruments, including Commission-2024/Pr.Kr.60/Mavak resolutions of 6 August 2024 and Commission-2024/Pr.Kr.232/Mavak of 24 September 2024.
- **Controlling-source warning:** Paragraph 3 expressly calls the list a compilation and directs that the original Government Resolution/order adding a community is to be relied on when determining the community and applying category benefits. The circular is therefore a useful current reference, not a substitute for the underlying orders.

The circular includes 20 earlier references. Those referenced orders were not individually opened and verified here; their titles, full text, and current effect should not be inferred from the reference list alone.

### 4.2 Scheduled Castes

- **Official administrative list:** [List of Scheduled Castes in Maharashtra (Total 59 Castes)](https://socialwelfare.maharashtra.gov.in/en/list-of-scheduled-castes-in-maharashtra-total-59-castes/)
- **Issuing authority:** Commissionerate of Social Welfare, Government of Maharashtra.
- **Date/version:** No publication or last-updated date is displayed on the page.
- **Verification:** The page was opened and its list of 59 names inspected.
- **Limit:** This is an official departmental list page, but it is not itself the underlying Presidential Order or a dated amending instrument. It is not enough to establish the current legal schedule or the effect of every alias, area restriction, or amendment.

### 4.3 Scheduled Tribes

- **Official Maharashtra Tribal Development Department page:** [Scheduled Tribes list / constitutional provisions](https://tribal.maharashtra.gov.in/1109/Provisions?Doctype=834f76f5-922c-4fb2-80e5-27d2132675ce)
- **Issuing authority:** Tribal Development Department, Government of Maharashtra.
- **What the page lists:** The page titled “अनुसूचित जमातींची यादी” lists the Constitution (Scheduled Tribes) Order, 1950, and historical amendment instruments, including 1956, 1976 and 2002 materials, with government-hosted PDF links.
- **Verification and currency:** The page and its listed entries were opened. Its footer states “last reviewed” 21 April 2015. It is useful for locating the historic instrument chain but is too old to establish that it is a complete current schedule in 2026. A current consolidated, authoritative Maharashtra ST schedule and all later amendments were **not verified**.

### 4.4 Status of the caste-law and schedule research

The verified 2012 Act compilation, 2012 Rules, 2025 VJNT/NT/OBC/SBC circular, and official SC list page are useful starting points. They do **not** close the legal gap completely:

1. The original GR cited as Form G and Form T remains unidentified.
2. The 2025 VJNT/NT/OBC/SBC circular itself requires reliance on the underlying GR/order for inclusion decisions; those originals need individual verification.
3. The SC administrative list has no displayed version date; its underlying central order and amendments were not opened here.
4. The ST department page is marked last reviewed in 2015; a current consolidated schedule remains unverified.
5. A consolidated Act/Rules text demonstrably current through 2026 was not located.

## 5. Maharashtra domicile residence period

### Evidence checked

- The [official combined Age, Nationality and Domicile application form](https://revenue.mahaonline.gov.in/Site/Common/Documents/Downloads/Application_form_for_Age_Nationality_and%20Domicile_Certificate.pdf) asks when the applicant began living at the current address, since when they have resided in Maharashtra, and about migration and (in a conditional section) a spouse's residence history.
- The form's header cites Form T of the 2012 GR reference described in section 2 above. Its PDF metadata dates to April 2014.
- The [Aaple Sarkar notified-services directory](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/en/CommonForm/ViewAllServices) lists the combined service, its designated officer and service time, but the public directory entry does not state a minimum number of years of residence.
- The official GR portal was opened, but the original Form T GR could not be retrieved through its CAPTCHA-gated search interface.

### Conclusion

No verified official source inspected in this research states a universal minimum residence period for issuance of this Maharashtra certificate. The form's request for residence dates is not itself a prescribed threshold. In particular, a commonly repeated “15 years” rule is **not established by the sources verified here** and should not be encoded as a general Maharashtra certificate-issuance requirement without the controlling GR or another current official instrument.

This conclusion is limited to the public official sources inspected; it is not proof that no separate rule, scheme-specific condition, or case-specific instruction exists.

## 6. Separate Maharashtra nationality-only certificate

The official Aaple Sarkar notified-services directory lists **Age, Nationality and Domicile Certificate** as one combined Revenue service (15 days; Tahsildar). No separate service titled only “Nationality Certificate” appeared in the directory checked. The public Revenue application form is also a combined form.

- **Directory:** [Aaple Sarkar notified services](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/en/CommonForm/ViewAllServices)
- **Combined service detail:** [Age, Nationality and Domicile Certificate, ServiceId 1253](https://aaplesarkar.mahaonline.gov.in/en/Login/Certificate_Documents?ServiceId=1253)
- **Finding:** The official public service catalogue supports describing the Maharashtra service found as combined. It does not prove that no separate nationality-related document or administrative process exists outside that catalogue.
- **Important distinction:** The [Government of India's Indian Citizenship Online portal](https://indiancitizenshiponline.nic.in/) concerns statutory citizenship processes under central law; it is not evidence that Maharashtra has a nationality-only certificate service and should not be conflated with the combined state certificate.

## 7. Certificate specimens, QR/barcode and digital-signature verification

### Public verification page found

- **Official Revenue portal:** [Bar Code Verification](https://revenue.mahaonline.gov.in/verify/)
- **Issuing authority:** Government of Maharashtra Revenue Department's Maha e-Seva portal.
- **What was opened:** The page is titled “Bar Code Verification” and presents a barcode input and a CAPTCHA.
- **What it does not establish:** The public page does not explain barcode/QR formats, provide a sample certificate, describe how to validate a digital signature, or show the result without a user-supplied barcode and CAPTCHA. No attempt was made to submit a real certificate or bypass the CAPTCHA.

### Specimens and digital signatures

No official specimen of an issued Income, Caste, Domicile, or Nationality certificate, and no Maharashtra-certificate-specific QR or digital-signature validation manual, was verified in the sources checked. The application forms are application templates, not certificate specimens. The 2025 community-list circular says that circular is digitally signed; this does not establish verification instructions for citizen certificates.

**Safe ingestion position:** Keep the barcode verification page as a pointer to an official validation mechanism, but do not claim that a QR code or digital signature is validated unless the relevant certificate-specific official instructions are obtained and inspected.

## 9. Remaining research gaps and next steps

1. Obtain the actual 2012 GR for Appendix B Forms G and T through an accessible official copy or a normal request to the issuing department; capture its title, date, amendments, and appendices before interpreting it.
2. Locate an authoritative current consolidated version of the caste Act and Rules, and verify any post-2012 amendments against the Maharashtra Gazette or the issuing department.
3. Follow the 2025 VJNT/NT/OBC/SBC circular's cited original orders—especially the 2024 references—and verify each order's scope and continuing effect.
4. Obtain current controlling SC and ST schedule instruments, including all amendments applicable to Maharashtra; do not treat the undated SC webpage or 2015-reviewed ST portal page as a complete current legal schedule.
5. Verify the domicile threshold question against the original Form T GR and current Revenue Department instructions. Until then, record the minimum period as **not established by verified evidence**.
6. Ask the Revenue Department for a public issued-certificate specimen and written barcode/QR/digital-signature verification steps, if they are intended for a future document-ingestion corpus.
